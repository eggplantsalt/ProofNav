# M3 candidate mechanism tournament

## Frozen problem and surviving claim

VLN-NF already collides with any claim to first introduce false-premise VLN,
evidence exploration, FOUND/NOT-FOUND or a paired REVERIE-like task.  Its ROAM
baseline performs room navigation and LLM/VLM-guided in-room exploration
([Su et al., ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1152/)).
ProofNav's remaining research opportunity is narrower:

> couple typed proof obligations and active evidence collection to a causal,
> independently checked terminal certificate whose risk is calibrated for the
> complete stopping policy.

The M3 tournament asks which minimum mechanism repairs frozen DUET's current
false SUPPORTs without GT or future information.

## Why terminal-cut won this round

It was the smallest causal modification that explained all exposed premature
counterexamples, used no new labels/weights, did not invent independence and
had a frozen scan-disjoint confirmation.  The confirmatory result passed the
precommitted directional gate.  It therefore wins as the M3-B mechanism probe,
not as the final paper algorithm.

It did **not** pass the strict risk or completeness gate.  One STOP-time purse
identity error remains and the best-case hypothetical 95% bound is `0.582`.
Consequently, the champion is a necessary timing primitive inside the future
method, while the unique next scientific investment is a typed null-aware
grounding model followed by proof-obligation-guided continuation.

## Collision matrix

| Neighbor | Directly covers | Gap ProofNav may investigate |
|---|---|---|
| VLN-NF / ROAM | false-premise instructions, explicit NOT-FOUND, exploration and coverage-aware task metric | machine-checkable causal certificate; calibrated whole-policy terminal risk; typed proof obligations |
| selective classification / SelectiveNet | score threshold, reject option, risk--coverage learning | embodied correlated evidence and verifier-gated stopping; generic rejection is not novel |
| RCPS / CRC / LTT | finite-sample risk control, expected monotone loss, multi-parameter selection | definition and control of the complete adaptive certificate event; standard theorem remains prior art |
| [conformal object detection](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v204/andeol23a.html) | box/label coverage under detector assumptions; proposal miss is a known impossibility mode | honest residual OPEN and active acquisition under missing proposals, not box expansion |
| [FGR2R / sub-instruction VLN](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.271/) | sub-instruction grounding and path fidelity | grounding predicates as proof obligations with refusal/termination semantics |
| [Open3DSG](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Koch_Open3DSG_Open-Vocabulary_3D_Scene_Graphs_from_Point_Clouds_with_Queryable_CVPR_2024_paper.html) | open-vocabulary objects and relations from 3D point clouds | current ProofNav lacks RGB-D/point clouds; adding typed relations is not novel by itself |
| proof-carrying/certificate systems | checker-separated claims | grounding and exploration semantics in false-premise VLN; schema alone is engineering |

No candidate is accepted as novelty merely because it combines known pieces.
Publication requires a new active policy/structural result, a genuinely useful
typed grounding capability, or a fair complete-cost advantage under the full
terminal loss.
